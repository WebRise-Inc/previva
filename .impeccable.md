## Design Context

### Users

Previva serves individuals, families, professionals, and public-sector employees in the Montréal and Laval area. Visitors use the website to understand financial-planning and insurance services, evaluate whether Previva fits their needs, access client resources, and book or prepare a conversation with an advisor.

### Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and clear. The experience should feel reassuring and personal without becoming casual, playful, or overly corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Previva blue (`#212BC2`) with deep blue and quiet neutral surfaces. Preserve the clean editorial structure inspired by the SureTrust reference while using Previva’s own content and identity. Avoid green, gradients, decorative dashboard patterns, excessive animation, and layouts that sacrifice readability for oversized typography.

### Design Principles

1. Let clarity and confidence lead every layout and copy decision.
2. Scale typography according to both viewport width and content length.
3. Keep navigation intact until the available width genuinely requires the mobile menu.
4. Maintain reliable blue-white contrast across every interactive state.
5. Make French and English layouts equally intentional, with no clipped navigation or awkward wrapping.
